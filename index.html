    Corrections applied automatically

    1. Rows that have an item name but no other details are removed. Rows with at least one value are kept.
    2. Day Consumption is converted from tons to kg, and No. of Days Consumption is recalculated using kg.
    3. Raw Aging Stock: Lot Code, Lot No, Supplier Lot No, Category, GSM, Qty and Machine Line are removed. Rows are sorted by Holding Days (highest first) and the quantity column (QTN) is renamed "QTY (kg)".
    4. All TagEnv Aging Stock: Lot Code, Lot No, Supplier Lot No, Width, GSM and Source Type are removed. Repeated items are grouped, with total quantity (QTY and Qty columns) and average Holding Days. Click the item (or the [+] in Excel) to see each record.
    5. Decimal numbers are rounded to whole numbers.
    6. Zero values are shown as "-" on every sheet.
    7. Headers, alignment, column widths, borders and frozen header rows are applied to every sheet.
    Sheet order and names are kept as in the original file.
